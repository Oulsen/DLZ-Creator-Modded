# Changelog

## v0.1.0

- (DLZ BUG) Recording crashes after take 9,999
- (DLZ BUG) Anyone on the network, or a prepared SD card or USB stick, can change files on the mixer
- (DLZ BUG) A new recording can overwrite an older one
- (FEATURE) Install the mod on your own mixer yourself, with a step-by-step guide
- (FEATURE) Update the mod without losing recordings or settings
- (FEATURE) Connect to Wi-Fi from the screen (needs a USB Wi-Fi stick, the mixer has no Wi-Fi of its own)
- (FEATURE) Modded SD card with its own startup screens
- (FEATURE) Firmware check: only 1.2.0 (build 1731) is supported
- (FEATURE) "DLZ Creator Modded" tab in Settings
- (FEATURE) Option to hide the header bar at the top of the screen, for more room
- (FEATURE) Mod's SD card also holds recordings; "Format SD" is blocked
- (FEATURE) Safety locks: no firmware updates from the card, internal storage untouched
- (FEATURE) Unique recording names, like REC_0003_otter, numbered across SD card and USB
- (FEATURE) Recordings go to the SD card by default
- (FEATURE) Startup log on the SD card, for bug reports
- (FEATURE) Better meters: level before and after the fader (choice in the mod settings), yellow and red zones, dB scale
- (FEATURE) Turn remote login (SSH) on from the screen with your own password, off by default
- (FEATURE) Back up a mixer on other firmware, so the mod can support it
- (FEATURE) The mod also works on the full-size DLZ Creator
- (FEATURE) Faster install
